# virtualmase Root Property — Design Direction

## Three possible approaches

| Theme name | Very brief intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| **Signal Library** | A warm-white editorial directory shaped by notations, field labels, and a navigable systems map. It should feel like a respected research index rather than a startup home page. | 0.07 |
| **Civic Switchboard** | A public-service-style routing surface with typographic clarity, practical prompts, and rigorous visible boundaries. The emotional intent is calm competence rather than spectacle. | 0.03 |
| **Archive in Motion** | A spare chronicle of public work using timelines and documentation fragments. It emphasizes provenance and evolution. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Signal Library

**Design movement.** Contemporary editorial information design, informed by Swiss wayfinding systems, library finding aids, and technical field notebooks.

**Core principles.** The root property is a directory, not a duplicated flagship. Every compositional choice must clarify which distinct public property answers a reader’s question. Typography creates hierarchy before decoration; source paths remain visibly available; and spacious asymmetry makes routes feel intentional rather than templated.

**Color philosophy.** Paper white communicates legibility and restraint. Ink blue gives the page its working authority. Ultramarine is an owned navigation signal—not a background effect—while a reserved oxide-orange marker distinguishes a condition, note, or unresolved boundary. The palette is deliberately light to make detailed reading comfortable and to keep the distinct dark AI Mastery property visually separate.

**Layout paradigm.** A reading desk meets a route map: a narrow left metadata rail, a large asymmetric entry field, then offset route panels that alternate their anchoring edge. The path is a sequence of reader questions, not a generic centered-card grid.

**Signature elements.** A four-node property atlas, numbered route coordinates, and outlined status lozenges appear throughout. Lines are not decoration: they connect a question to an appropriate property.

**Interaction philosophy.** The only interaction is useful navigation. The compact mobile drawer preserves the document’s route hierarchy; route tiles gain a small directional shift only when a reader intentionally hovers or focuses them. Nothing is collected, stored, or inferred about a visitor.

**Animation.** Motion is opt-in and quiet: the property atlas may settle in on first view with opacity and transform transitions under 280 ms. Route tiles lift by two pixels on pointer hover. Keyboard navigation is instant, and all nonessential motion is removed when reduced motion is requested.

**Typography system.** Decision voice: Iowan Old Style, Baskerville, and Georgia. Working language: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. Trace labels: SFMono-Regular, Consolas, Liberation Mono, monospace. Headings are tightly set serif statements; route labels are spaced uppercase mono; paragraphs stay within a readable measure.

**Brand essence.** virtualmase is a public index for independent systems work, for readers who need to choose a credible starting point rather than consume a funnel. **Clear, bounded, investigative.**

**Brand voice.** Headlines are direct, editorial, and specific. Calls to action describe the reader’s next inspection task rather than issuing generic commands. Example lines: “Choose the question before you choose the route.” and “Inspect the work record, not a claim about the work.”

**Wordmark and logo.** A compact `V/` directory mark: two converging diagonal routes crossing a single reference axis. The sign appears as a compact glyph beside the deliberately constructed “virtualmase” wordmark, never as plain default text alone.

**Signature brand color.** **Atlas Ultramarine — `#1746C8`**.
